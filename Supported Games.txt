UN64 Patcher - Supported Games by Feature
=========================================
Updated 2026-11-23. 46 games can be patched; 4 more are recognised but not patchable yet.
All ROMs must be unmodified USA dumps (.z64, .v64 or .n64). Where a specific revision is
needed it is shown in brackets, e.g. (Rev 1). The original ROM is never changed - the
patched copy is saved next to it, or in the folder chosen with the Destination button.

All patches are EXPERIMENTAL and untested on real hardware unless noted otherwise.


1. ASPECT RATIO: 3:2, 16:10, 16:9 and 21:9  (27 games)
-------------------------------------------------------
The 3D camera is widened to the chosen aspect. Set your TV / emulator / Analogue 3D to
stretch the picture to the same aspect. HUD and 2D art are stretched along with it.

2. ASPECT RATIO: 4:3 (original)  (44 games)
--------------------------------------------
Keeps the game's own 4:3 picture and only applies the Resolution and/or Frame rate options
you pick. Works for every game in sections 3 and 4. Not available for Perfect Dark (it has
no resolution or frame-rate patch yet) or Mortal Kombat Trilogy (16:9 only). Choosing
4:3 + Original resolution + Original frame rate leaves a ROM unchanged, so no file is made.


3. RESOLUTION: 360p, 360i, 480i and 540i  (44 games)
----------------------------------------------------
480i: the game's video modes are switched to their interlaced 480i twins (the picture is
still drawn at 240 lines). 360p / 360i / 540i are EXPERIMENTAL re-scaled modes with a
non-standard refresh, built for the Analogue 3D's scaler - a plain TV may not sync.
"Original" leaves the video mode unchanged.

4. FRAME RATE: up to 60 fps / up to 40 fps  (5 games)
------------------------------------------------------
Raises how often the game presents a new frame. The ~60 Hz video retrace is the ceiling,
so overclocking (e.g. Analogue 3D Unleashed) helps the game reach the target but never
exceed it. Most games run their logic once per frame, so they also run faster as the frame
rate climbs (Mario Kart 64 is the exception). Up to 40 fps is the gentler option.

                                       60 fps   40 fps
  Diddy Kong Racing   (Rev 1)            yes      yes
  Gex 3: Deep Cover Gecko                yes      -
  Mario Kart 64                          yes      -     game speed stays correct at 60;
                                                        music may play fast
  Star Fox 64                            yes      yes
  Yoshi's Story                          yes      -
